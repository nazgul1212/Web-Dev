Recipe#1

  • Time: 60 minutes
  • Serves: Pakhlava
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 500 g
Eggs 2 pcs
Milk 150 g
Sugar 1 tp
Butter 50 g
Yest 15 g
Walnuts 250 g

Step 1:crack eggs and mix with butter and flour.

Step 2: Add the yeast and mix.

Step 3: Let the dough rest.

Step 4:Put the nuts through a meat grinder and mix with sugar or powdered sugar.

Step 5:Divide the risen dough into 12 pieces.

Step 6:Roll the dough onto rolling pin and transfer it to the pan.

Step 7:Grese the dough with the filling one by one.

Recipe#2

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: Easy Pizza
Ingredients Quantity
Pita 2 pcs
Sausage or another meat product 2-3 pcs
Any Sauce 10 g
Tomatos 1 pcs
Any cheese 200-300 g

Step 1:Сut the tomatoes and meat as you like.

Step 2: Grate the cheese.

Step 3: Put grated cheese to first pita and cover with the second pita.

Step 4:Spread the sauce on the second pita.

Step 5:Put the tomatos and meat to pita, then with remaining cheese sprinkle the our "pizza".

Step 6:Bake 5 minutes in oveт or frying pan w/o any oil.
